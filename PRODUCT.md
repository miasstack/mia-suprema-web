# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

New fans discovering Mia Suprema via Instagram and TikTok — cold traffic, mobile-first. Age 18–35, bilingual (Spanish / English), arriving from a short-form video. They have 10–20 seconds of curiosity. The secondary surface (index.html) serves the same audience after they've warmed up slightly; the bio page (bio.html) is the hard-conversion front door.

## Product Purpose

Artist hub for Mia Suprema: a Latin pop recording artist. The site serves three jobs in order of priority:

1. **Audience capture**: house retargeting pixels (Meta, TikTok) so every visitor can be reached again.
2. **Fan conversion**: move new visitors to music streams, social follows, and exclusive-content subscription ("For the Real Fans").
3. **Credibility signal**: look like a real, polished artist — not a hobbyist or a template.

The bio page (bio.html) is the primary conversion surface; index.html is the deeper artist story for warmed-up visitors.

## Brand Personality

Luxury Latin pop. Dark, sensual, high fashion. The references are closer to a YSL or Dior editorial than a pop-star fan site. Three words: **cinematic, refined, heat**.

Voice is confident and minimal — she doesn't explain herself. Copy is short, Spanish-inflected when appropriate, never chatty.

## Anti-references

- **Try-hard edgy**: neon on black, graffiti fonts, SoundCloud-rapper aesthetics — she is luxury, not street.
- **Safe corporate**: bland major-label polish, generic Spotify artist page templates, forgettable stock-photo layouts.
- **Generic link-in-bio**: flat Linktree-style buttons, white/pastel backgrounds, the obvious template read.
- **Tropical stereotype**: bright reds and oranges, flag imagery, folkloric signifiers — she is high fashion, not regional.

## Design Principles

1. **Luxury through restraint** — premium feel comes from what's removed, not added. Dark palette, generous negative space, controlled typography.
2. **Conversion without aggression** — every link and CTA should feel like an invitation, not a push. The "For the Real Fans" placement is the primary signal, not a popup.
3. **Mobile native** — designed on the phone screen first. Nothing adapted down; everything built up.
4. **Heat implied, never stated** — sensuality lives in image choice, slow reveal animations, and dark-romantic color temperature. Never explicit, never crude.
5. **Pixel-first architecture** — the bio page must load the retargeting pixel immediately, before any other script, to maximize audience capture from bounce traffic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Minimum WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on all text over dark backgrounds.
- Touch targets minimum 44×44px on all interactive elements (critical: mobile-first surface).
- Reduced motion: respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for entrance animations.
- No accessibility-specific user needs noted beyond standard mobile web baseline.
